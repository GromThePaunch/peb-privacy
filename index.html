Peb privacy policy

Effective October 14, 2026 · covers Peb 0.8.4 and later · applies to the Peb app for Android (com.mmmadigan.peb)

The short version

  • Peb reads your daily steps (and, if you allow it, exercise sessions) from Health Connect, only to feed and grow your pet.
  • Your steps, health data and pet stay on your phone. Peb has no account and no ads.
  • If you leave "Share anonymous usage and crash data" on (it's on by default), Peb sends anonymous app-usage events (like "transformed" or "played a mini-game") and crash reports to Google Firebase, so we can fix bugs and see which features get used. Never your steps, health data, step goal or pet's name. Turn it off any time in Settings.
  • Peb never sells or shares your data, never uploads your health data, and never writes anything to Health Connect.
  • You can turn access off at any time, and uninstalling Peb deletes everything it stored.

Who makes Peb

Peb is made by the independent developer TheMadMadigan ("we"). Questions about this policy: hello@themadmadigan.net.

What Peb reads from Health Connect, and why

DataWhy Peb uses itRequired?
Steps (daily totals, last 14 days)Your steps fill Peb's food bowl, grow Peb up, charge its head plants for transformations, and power your daily goal, Sun Stones, walking streaks, decorations and the home-screen widget.Yes. Peb is a step pet.

Peb only asks for these after you tap to connect, using Android's Health Connect permission screen. Peb does not read heart rate, sleep, weight, location or any other health data, and it never writes data to Health Connect.

Peb's use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your health data is used only to provide Peb's features to you. It is never used for advertising, never sold, never shared with or transferred to anyone, and never read by humans.

What Peb stores, and where

Peb keeps its data in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • your pet (name, stats, journal, collected plants, forms, decorations, game scores), your daily step goal, and the Peb Pile of retired Pebs;
  • a short step history (about the last 4 weeks of daily totals) so steps are never counted twice;
  • your settings (sound, notification choices) and a small summary the home-screen widget shows.

None of this is sent anywhere. Peb opts out of Android cloud backup, so it isn't copied to Google Drive either. Moving it directly to a new phone (device-to-device transfer) still works.

Anonymous usage and crash data (Firebase)

Peb uses Google's Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They are on by default, and only while the Share anonymous usage and crash data setting is on (Journal → bell tab). Turning it off stops both right away.

  • App activity: a short list of in-app events: app opened, egg hatched, plant bloomed (its rarity), Peb transformed (the form, life stage, and whether a secret recipe was used), plant swapped, Peb grew (the stage), Peb retired (generation and form), a mini-game or tidy-up was played (the game and a rough score range like "75–89%"), a Sun Stone was earned, and the step goal was changed (only a range like "8k–10k", never the exact goal). Firebase also records basic session events (first open, session start, time in app).
  • Crash logs and diagnostics: if Peb crashes, a crash report with the error, the app version, device model and Android version.
  • Device or other IDs: Firebase uses a random app-instance ID (and Crashlytics an installation ID) to group events from the same install. These are not your name, email, phone number or advertising ID, and reset if you reinstall or clear Peb's data.

Never sent: step counts, exercise sessions or any other Health Connect data, your exact step goal, your pet's name, your journal, save codes, or anything you type. Peb does not collect the advertising ID, turns off ad personalization and ad storage, does not link Firebase to any ad service, and does not set a user ID. Firebase may infer an approximate country/region from the connection, as Google Analytics does for all apps.

This data is sent encrypted (HTTPS) to Google, which processes it for us as a service provider under the Firebase data processing terms. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else. Analytics event data is kept for 2 months and crash reports for 90 days, then deleted. To ask for deletion of this data, email us.

No other collection or sharing

Apart from the optional usage and crash data above, nothing leaves your device. Peb has no sign-in, no advertising and no tracking across apps. The app's only built-in links open web pages you choose, like Health Connect's Play Store page.

Save codes (optional)

In Settings you can copy a "save code" to move your pet to another install. The code contains your pet's save, including recent daily step totals. It is created only when you tap the button and goes only where you paste it. Treat it like a private note.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Peb sends at most two gentle reminders a day (for example "your bowl is full" or "your streak needs a stroll"), never between 9:30 PM and 8 AM. They're created on your phone from your step data. You can turn each type off in Peb's settings, or turn them all off in Android settings.

Your choices

  • Revoke health access any time: Android Settings → Apps → Health Connect → App permissions → Peb (on some phones: Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Health Connect).
  • Stop usage and crash data: turn off Share anonymous usage and crash data in Peb's settings (Journal → bell tab).
  • Delete your data: clear Peb's storage in Android settings, or uninstall Peb. Both remove everything Peb stored on your phone. Anonymous usage and crash data already sent expires on the schedule above, or email us to ask for deletion sooner.

Children

Peb is meant for a general audience aged 13 and up, and it isn't designed for children under 13. Peb does not knowingly collect personal data from children; the optional usage and crash data is anonymous and never includes health data.

Security

Your data stays in Android's private app storage, which other apps can't read. Health data and your pet are never transmitted. Usage and crash data travels to Firebase over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Changes

If Peb ever handles data differently, this page will be updated before the change ships, with a new effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
